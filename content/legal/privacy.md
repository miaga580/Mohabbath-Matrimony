This Privacy Policy explains how Miaga Technologies LLP ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Mohabbath Matrimony mobile application and website (the "Platform"). Mohabbath Matrimony is published and operated by **Miaga Technologies LLP**.

*Effective date: 23 November 2026*

---

### 1. What Data is Collected

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with secure, personalized matrimonial matchmaking services:

- **Account Credentials & Contact Data:** Full name, phone number, email address, date of birth, and gender.
- **Profile Details:** Marital status, height, education, occupation, annual income range, current location/residence, family details, religion, community, sect, languages spoken, and partner preferences.
- **Photos:** Profile pictures and identification images voluntarily uploaded to your profile.
- **Location Information:** Approximate or precise location, only if you explicitly permit it in your device settings, used to suggest nearby matches.
- **Device & Usage Info:** Device model, operating system, app version, IP address, device identifiers, app activity logs, and feature usage to ensure app integrity, prevent fraud, and optimize performance.

---

### 2. How Data is Used

We use your data responsibly to:
- Create, authenticate, and manage your account via OTP verification;
- Provide matchmaking recommendations, profile discovery, and mutual connection features;
- Enable communications, contact requests, and interactions between verified users;
- Provide prompt customer support and resolve user inquiries or safety grievances;
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, harassment, fake profiles, and objectionable behavior;
- Comply with applicable legal and statutory requirements.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

**We never sell your personal data.** 

We only share personal data with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the platform under strict confidentiality agreements:
- **SMS & Communication Providers:** (e.g., MSG91) for delivery of authentication OTPs, SMS, and WhatsApp alerts;
- **Payment Processors:** (e.g., Razorpay, Apple In-App Purchase) for securely processing premium subscription transactions without us storing your card or banking credentials;
- **Cloud Hosting & Infrastructure:** (e.g., Amazon Web Services) for highly secure, encrypted database hosting and cloud storage;
- **Law Enforcement & Legal Authorities:** Only when strictly required by applicable law, court order, or official governmental request.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Your Right to Delete Data

We retain your personal information only for as long as your account remains active or as required to fulfill legal, tax, accounting, and security obligations.

- **Account & Data Deletion:** You have the absolute right to delete your account and personal data at any time directly within the Mohabbath app under **Settings > Delete Account**, or by submitting a request via our [Data Deletion](/data-deletion) page.
- **Processing Time:** Once deleted, your profile, photos, and messages are immediately hidden from other users, and all associated personal data is permanently removed or anonymized within 30 days, except where retention is strictly required by statutory laws.

---

### 5. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse:
- Data transmitted between the app, website, and servers is encrypted in transit using modern TLS/SSL encryption.
- Cloud databases are encrypted at rest with stringent role-based access controls.
- Continuous vulnerability monitoring and security audits.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy (Under 18 Not Allowed)

Mohabbath Matrimony is exclusively intended for adults of legal marriageable age. **Individuals under 18 years of age are strictly prohibited from creating an account or using the app.** We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that an account belongs to a minor, the account and all associated data will be immediately terminated.
